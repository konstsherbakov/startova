How a launch runs

  1. 1

    Call

    30 minutes on WeChat, Zoom or at the expo. We learn your product, terms and limits.

  2. 2

    Market brief

    In 2–3 weeks: demand, competitors, prices and the best two or three cities for your category.

  3. 3

    Partner and adaptation

    We bring two or three vetted candidates and adapt the product, price and materials.

  4. 4

    Launch

    First unit or first shipment, local promotion and reports on how it sells.
